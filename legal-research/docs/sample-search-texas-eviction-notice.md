## Answer

**Recent change:** Senate Bill 38 (89th Leg., R.S., 2025) rewrote parts of this section effective **January 1, 2026**, and repealed subsections (f) through (i). Guides written before 2026 may describe delivery rules that no longer apply. [1]

1. **Lease or oral rental agreement.** Before filing a forcible detainer (eviction) suit against a tenant who defaulted or held over, the landlord must give **at least three days' written notice to vacate**, unless a written lease sets a different period. The section adds specific requirements when the lease is ended for nonpayment. If the suit is based on holding over, it must also meet Property Code § 91.001. [1, § 24.005(a)]
2. **Tenant at will or by sufferance.** Three days' written notice, unless a written agreement says otherwise. **After a foreclosure or tax sale**, a residential tenant who pays on time and isn't otherwise in default gets **30 days' written notice** if the buyer chooses not to continue the lease. [1, § 24.005(b)]
3. **Tenant of someone who took possession by forcible entry.** Three days' written notice. [1, § 24.005(c)]
4. **Federal notice rules.** A landlord who meets this section doesn't have to delay filing because of a federal notice requirement. However, a writ of possession can't be served until any federal notice period has run. [1, § 24.005(c-1)]
5. **Forcible entry.** The person entitled to possession must give oral or written notice to vacate before filing a forcible entry and detainer suit. [1, § 24.005(d)]
6. **Notice combined with an opportunity to respond.** If a lease or law requires an opportunity to respond, the notice periods can run at the same time. [1, § 24.005(e)]
7. **How the notice must be delivered.**
    - By mail, including certified or registered mail or a delivery service.
    - Inside the premises in a conspicuous place.
    - By hand to a person 16 or older at the premises.
    - Electronically, if the parties agreed to that in writing.

    These delivery rules don't apply if the tenant actually received the notice. [1, § 24.005(f-3), (f-4)]

**Related sections in the same chapter:** § 24.0042 (Computation of Time), § 24.00505 (Petition), § 24.0051 (Suit to Evict and Recover Unpaid Rent), § 24.0061 (Writ of Possession). These are not summarized here and are linked for further reading. [1]
